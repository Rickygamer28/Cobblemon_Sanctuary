# Cobblemon: Sanctuary

A NeoForge addon for **Cobblemon 1.8.4** (Minecraft 1.21.1) that gives your PC Pokémon a living home.

Place a **Sanctuary Core** and every Pokémon in your PC moves into a little world: a Normal-type hub in the middle and a ring of 16 type biomes around it, ordered so common dual types share a border. The land reshapes itself into the biomes over a few minutes, a little differently every time, and each biome grows as you collect more Pokémon of that type.

## Requirements
- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1
- Cobblemon 1.8.4 (NeoForge)
- Kotlin for Forge (already needed by Cobblemon)

## Getting started
1. Craft a **Sanctuary Core**: Cobblemon PC in the middle, Pasture Blocks on the 4 sides, any Type Gem in the corners.
2. Place it in an open area. An outline shows the area (white) and the most it can grow to (blue). Choose a size and press **Confirm**.
3. You get a **Sanctuary Journal**. Use it to read about your sanctuary; right-click a resident with it to pet, play, feed, see its info, change its home, or take it with you.

## What's in this version (0.1.0)
- Sanctuary Core with preview, size choice and free expansion (Medium at 100 Pokémon, Large at 300).
- 16 type biomes plus the Normal hub, each with 3 random variants, blended borders, growing with your collection.
- Every PC Pokémon is a resident; up to 60 are out at once near players (configurable).
- Homes follow the typing of the final evolution; Set Home picks between the Pokémon's own types.
- Dual types hang around shared borders or commute through the hub; Flying types fly in flocks and sometimes fly out over other biomes.
- Pokémon meet, greet, nuzzle, play-fight and have standoffs using their own animations; friendships and rivalries form over time.
- Whistle (call a resident), Hub Bell (everyone gathers in the hub for a while), invites to share a sanctuary.
